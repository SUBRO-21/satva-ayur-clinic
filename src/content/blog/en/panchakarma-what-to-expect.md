Many people hear about Panchakarma but are unsure what it involves day to day. This guide walks through a typical programme.

## Before you begin

Every programme starts with a consultation. The doctor assesses your constitution (*prakriti*), current imbalance (*vikriti*) and health history, and decides whether Panchakarma is suitable now.

## Preparation: snehana and swedana

For several days you take medicated ghee internally and receive oil massage and herbal steam. This prepares the body for the main procedure.

## The main procedure

Depending on your condition, this may be *virechana* (purgation), *basti* (medicated enema) or another procedure. It is always supervised.

## Afterwards: samsarjana krama

A gradual return to normal food over several days. This step matters as much as the procedure itself.

## How to prepare

- Keep your schedule light during the programme.
- Tell the doctor about any medicines you take.
- Bring loose, comfortable clothes.
