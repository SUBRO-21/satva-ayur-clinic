Ayurveda places as much weight on what you do every day as on any treatment. *Dinacharya* — the daily routine — is a set of habits meant to keep digestion, sleep and energy steady.

## 1. Wake before sunrise

Classical texts recommend rising during *brahma muhurta*, roughly 90 minutes before sunrise. If that is not practical, aim for the same waking time every day.

## 2. Clean the mouth and senses

Scrape the tongue, brush, and rinse the mouth. *Gandusha* (oil pulling) with sesame oil is a traditional addition.

## 3. Abhyanga — oil massage

A few minutes of warm-oil self-massage before bathing. The oil is chosen by constitution and season; ask at your consultation which suits you.

## 4. Regular meals

Eat the largest meal at midday, when digestion is strongest, and keep dinner light and early.

## 5. An early night

Aim to be asleep by 10 pm. Screens off an hour before bed helps.
