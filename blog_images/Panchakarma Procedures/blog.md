Five Classical Panchakarma Procedures
Panchakarma represents the core bio-purification and detoxification system of traditional Ayurvedic medicine. Each classical procedure is tailored to specific physiological indications and dosha imbalances under clinical supervision.
1. Vamana – Therapeutic Emesis
A classical Ayurvedic procedure involving medically supervised therapeutic emesis. 
2. Virechana – Therapeutic Purgation
A supervised cleansing procedure .
3. Basti – Medicated Enema Therapy
A traditional Ayurvedic procedure involving the administration of prescribed medicated liquids or oils through the rectal route. 
4. Nasya – Nasal Therapy
Administration of prescribed medicinal preparations through the nasal route .
5. Raktamokshana – Bloodletting Therapy
A specialized traditional procedure that involves the controlled removal of a small quantity of blood.
Panchakarma Therapies Available at Satva Ayur Health Center
At Satva Ayur Health Centre, therapies are customized based on individual health assessments, seasonal factors, and specific wellness goals.
1. Abhyanga – Ayurvedic Oil Massage
Abhyanga is a traditional full-body massage using suitable, individually specified herbal oils.

Promotes relaxation: Calms the central nervous system and relieves daily fatigue.
Helps relieve muscular tension: Improves joint mobility and relaxes tight muscle tissue.
Supports a sense of physical comfort: Mobilizes systemic circulation and aids toxin elimination.
Nourishes and moisturizes the skin: Tones the cutaneous layers and enhances complexion radiance.
2. Shirodhara – Relaxing Oil Therapy
Shirodhara is a deeply restorative treatment involving the continuous, gentle pouring of warm medicated oil, herbal buttermilk (Takra), or milk over the forehead.

Deep mental relaxation: Calms overactive mental processing and reduces stress markers.
Supports nervous system health: Highly effective in managing anxiety, tension headaches, and mood disturbances.
Improves sleep quality: Encourages deep rest and helps relieve chronic insomnia.
Nourishes scalp and sensory organs: Strengthens hair roots and relieves strain around the eyes and head.
3. Udvartana – Herbal Powder Massage
Udvartana is a specialized therapeutic massage executed utilizing prescribed dry herbal powders.

Assists lymphatic drainage: Supports metabolism and enhances lymphatic flow.
Exfoliates and revitalizes skin: Cleanses clogged pores and restores cutaneous softness.
Relieves muscular stiffness: Stimulates subcutaneous tissues to ease physical tension.
Balances Kapha dosha: Aids weight management and complements healthy regimen goals.
